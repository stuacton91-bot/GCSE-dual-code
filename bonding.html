Metallic bonding

＋ ＋ ＋Metallic bond
iMetal atoms lose their outer electrons. The electrons become delocalised — free to move between the fixed positive ions.
  • ⚡Conducts electricity — the free electrons carry charge. Press Pass a current.
  • 🔨Malleable — layers of ions can slide without breaking the bond.
  • 🌡High melting point — strong attraction between ions and the electron sea.
