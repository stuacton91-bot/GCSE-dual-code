02 — The approach

Built for the hardest barriers. Better for everyone.

Five non-negotiable principles sit behind every tool. They come from SEND practice — and they happen to be good design for all learners.

01

One idea at a time

Cognitive load is the enemy. Nothing new appears until you ask for it — progressive disclosure over density, always.

02

The visual carries meaning

Every animation encodes something the words can’t — a sequence, a proportion, a transformation. No decorative motion.

03

Clear boundaries

Every region has a visible edge. A learner never has to guess where one thing ends and the next begins.

04

Calm & reversible

Nothing moves unless you trigger it. Everything can be undone. No timers, no surprises, no penalties.

05

Plain language

Short instructions, key term first. Subject vocabulary is taught on first use, never assumed.

03 — Accessibility, by default

Designed to pass, not patched to comply.

Accessibility isn’t a setting here — it’s the starting point. Every tool honours the reader’s own preferences and works without a mouse.

  • ✓AAA contrast on every piece of text
  • ✓Full keyboard control with visible focus
  • ✓Honours reduced-motion, reduced-transparency & high-contrast
  • ✓Touch targets ≥ 48px; works at 200% zoom
  • ✓Live announcements for screen readers
