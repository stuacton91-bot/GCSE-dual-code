Half-life · two angles

How fast does a sample decay?

The half-life is the average time for half of the radioactive nuclei in a sample to decay — the same time it takes the activity (count rate) to halve. Look at it two ways: read it off a decay graph, then predict the count rate after several half-lives with no graph at all.
